Free vs Premium Strategy

Free can be generous because it is read-only: viewer, deeper tables, quality checks, codes, UDFs, constraints, resources/cost summaries, comparison, dashboards, exports.

Premium AURORA should keep: XER writer/import validator, P6 API push/pull, OPC REST, SYNCHRO 4D automation, Power BI model generation, full time-phased EV/PV/AC and multi-project/server/AI workflows.
